ITEM 1 — List of Designated Persons (Updated as of 8 May 2026)
URL: https://www.amlo.go.th/index.php/whatsnew/detail/19220
Published: 14 May 2026 | Category: Sanctions/Designated Persons | Source: AMLO
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
AMLO updates the Section 7 designated persons list effective 8 May 2026.
Includes rights of designated persons to petition for delisting under
the Anti-Money Laundering Act B.E. 2542 (1999), Section 7.
Banks must screen customer and counterparty data against this updated list.

ITEM 2 — Transaction Committee Enforcement Action
URL: https://www.amlo.go.th/index.php/news-en/detail/18666
Published: 20 Mar 2026 | Category: Enforcement/Asset Seizure | Source: AMLO
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
The Transaction Committee (under AMLA B.E. 2542 Section 35) seized and
froze assets worth over THB 10,615 million linked to transnational
organised crime networks and scammer rings. Highlights heightened
enforcement posture. No direct regulatory obligation change; informs
risk appetite calibration.

ITEM 3 — FATF High Risk Jurisdictions (As of February 2026)
URL: https://www.amlo.go.th/index.php/whatsnew/detail/18802
Published: 16 Feb 2026 | Category: FATF Risk Jurisdictions | Source: AMLO
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Updated FATF grey list (Jurisdictions under Increased Monitoring) and
blacklist (Jurisdictions subject to a Call for Action) as of February 2026.
Banks must apply Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) for transactions involving
counterparties in listed jurisdictions. This supersedes the October 2025
and June 2025 versions.

FATF Call for Action (February 2026): Iran, North Korea (DPRK), Myanmar
FATF Increased Monitoring (February 2026 grey list additions/retentions):
  Algeria, Angola, Bulgaria, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Côte d'Ivoire,
  Croatia, Democratic Republic of Congo, Haiti, Kenya, Laos (Lao PDR),
  Lebanon, Mali, Monaco, Mozambique, Namibia, Nigeria, Philippines,
  South Africa, South Sudan, Syria, Tanzania, Venezuela, Vietnam,
  Yemen (changes reflect February 2026 plenary decisions)
